# Esplanade

Welcome to Esplanade!

> Esplanade (noun)
> 
> In French, esplanade is a feminine noun that means a wide, open, and flat space, public square, or paved promenade. 
> It often sits in front of a large building, a monument, or along a waterfront.
>
> Source: Gemini

### What is it?

Just like in the definition of the term, Esplanade is like a front of a building that leads to other places.

Concretely, it allows the user to stay organized by creating their own dashboard via the use of hyperlinks and iframes... it could be enough, but more options are coming!
